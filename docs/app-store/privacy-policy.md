# Mouselook privacy policy

Mouselook does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics or tracking.** The app has no sign-in of its own and
  sends nothing to the developer.
- **Websites you open.** Services such as Figma and GeForce NOW are loaded as
  their normal websites. When you sign in to them, their own privacy policies
  apply. Their cookies and sign-ins are stored on your iPad, inside the app.
- **Diagnostics.** The app keeps a short log of events (such as which service
  was opened and whether the mouse was captured) on your iPad. It leaves the
  device only if you tap "Copy diagnostics" and paste it somewhere yourself.
- **Microphone.** Used only when a website asks for it (for example voice chat
  in a game), after you allow it.
